# 🎯 Spear Phishing

---

## 📌 Definition
Spear phishing is a **targeted phishing attack** where the malicious actor invests time in collecting **OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)** information about the victim before launching the attack.  

This preparation makes the phishing email appear **more credible and personalized**, increasing the chances of success.  

---

## 🛠️ Characteristics
- Attackers research the victim using public sources (social media, company websites, forums).  
- Emails are **customized** with details relevant to the target (e.g., job role, projects, contacts).  
- Often used against **specific individuals or organizations** rather than broad mass campaigns.  
- Can be a precursor to **business email compromise (BEC)** or **credential theft**.  

---

## 🚨 Example
- A malicious actor finds out that Esteban works in cybersecurity.  
- They craft an email pretending to be from a **conference organizer**, including details about a recent event.  
- The email contains a malicious attachment disguised as a **workshop agenda**.  

---

## ✅ Summary
Spear phishing differs from regular phishing because it is **highly targeted and personalized**.  
By leveraging OSINT, attackers increase the **credibility of their emails**, making them harder to detect and more dangerous.
