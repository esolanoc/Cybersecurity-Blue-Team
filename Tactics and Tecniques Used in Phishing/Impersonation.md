# 🕵️ Impersonation

---

## 📌 Definition
Impersonation is the act of **pretending to be somebody else**.  
In cybersecurity, this tactic is often used to **gain trust** and trick victims into revealing sensitive information or performing actions that benefit the attacker.  

---

## 🛠️ Characteristics
- Attackers disguise themselves as a **trusted person or organization**.  
- Common in **phishing emails, phone calls, or fake websites**.  
- Often combined with **social engineering techniques** to increase credibility.  
- Can target individuals (e.g., employees) or entire organizations.  

---

## 🚨 Examples
- 📧 An attacker sends an email pretending to be the **CEO**, asking for urgent wire transfers.  
- 📞 A phone call where the attacker impersonates **IT support**, requesting login credentials.  
- 🌐 A fake website mimicking a **bank portal**, tricking users into entering account details.  

---

## ✅ Conclusion
Impersonation is a **deceptive technique** where attackers pose as someone else to manipulate victims.  
It is frequently used in **phishing campaigns** and is one of the most effective social engineering tactics.
