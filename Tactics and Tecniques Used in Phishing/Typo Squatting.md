# 🔠 Typo Squatting

---

## 📌 Definition
Typo squatting is the act of **impersonating a brand or domain name** by deliberately misspelling it.  
Attackers register domains that look similar to legitimate ones, tricking users into visiting malicious sites.  

---

## 🛠️ Examples
Below are domains that impersonate the real **SBT domain: `securityblue.team`**

- `securltyblue.team`  
- `securitybllue.team`  
- `securtyblue.team`  

---

## 🔍 Analysis
At first glance, these domains look legitimate.  
Only by focusing carefully can you spot the issues:  
- The **“I”** in *security* is replaced with a lowercase **“L”** → `securltyblue.team`  
- There are **two L’s** in *blue* → `securitybllue.team`  
- The **“I”** in *security* is missing → `securtyblue.team`  

---

## 🚨 Key Takeaway
Typo squatting exploits **human error** and **visual similarity**.  
It is commonly used in **phishing attacks** to steal credentials or distribute malware.  

---

## ✅ Summary
- Typo squatting = registering domains with **intentional misspellings**.  
- Goal: trick users into trusting malicious sites.  
- Defense: train users to verify URLs carefully and use **domain monitoring tools**.
