# 📜 Policies and Procedures

---

## 📌 Policy
A **policy** is a plan or course of action intended to guide decisions and actions within an organization.  
Policies establish **rules and expectations** for employees and define how the organization addresses specific domains.  

---

## 🛠️ Policy Examples
- **AUP (Acceptable Usage Policy)** → Defines what users can and cannot do within the organization’s systems.  
- **BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)** → Allows employees to use personal devices within the organization, under defined conditions.  
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** → Outlines the level of commitment and responsibilities between a **Service Provider (SP)** and the **Customer**.  

---

## 📑 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
A **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)** is a set of **step-by-step instructions** developed for a specific task.  
SOPs ensure consistency, efficiency, and compliance when performing operational activities.  

---

## ✅ Summary
- **Policies** → High-level plans that set rules and expectations.  
- **Examples** → AUP, BYOD, SLA.  
- **Procedures (SOPs)** → Detailed instructions for carrying out specific tasks.  
Together, policies and procedures provide a **framework for governance, compliance, and operational efficiency**.
