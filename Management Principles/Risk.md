# ⚠️ Risk

---

## 📌 Definition
Risk is a **negative impact** that an organization might face in areas such as:  
- 💼 Business  
- 💰 Financial  
- 🔐 Security  

---

## 🛠️ Risk Assessments
Risk assessments are conducted to **identify and determine the impact** of risks so organizations can make **informed decisions**.  

### 🔹 Conducting Assessments
1. Identify potential hazards.  
2. Identify what or who could be harmed by those hazards.  
3. Evaluate the risk based on **severity** and **likelihood**.  

---

## 🧩 Managing Risk
There are four main strategies to manage risk:  

- **Risk Avoidance** → Prevent the incident from happening.  
- **Risk Acceptance** → Proceed despite possible harm.  
- **Risk Mitigation** → Apply technical controls (patches, firewalls) or administrative controls (policies, procedures).  
- **Risk Transfer** → Shift the cost of potential loss to another party (e.g., insurance, outsourcing).  

---

## ✅ Conclusion
- Risk = potential **negative impact** on the organization.  
- Assessments help identify hazards, evaluate severity, and guide decisions.  
- Management strategies: **Avoid, Accept, Mitigate, Transfer**.
