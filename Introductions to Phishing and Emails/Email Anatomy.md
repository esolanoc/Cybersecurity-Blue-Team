# ✉️ Email Anatomy

---

## 📌 Email Structure
An email is composed of two main parts:  
- **Header**  
- **Body/Content**  

---

## 🧾 Headers
The **email header** is a set of lines containing metadata about the email, such as sender, recipient, and subject.  

### 🔹 Important Header Fields
- **From** → Sender’s address  
- **To** → Recipient’s address  
- **Date** → When the email was sent  

### 🔹 Optional Header Fields
- **Subject** → Topic of the email  
- **Reply-To** → Address for replies  
- **Message-ID** → Unique identifier for the email  
- **Received** → Path the email took through servers  

---

## 📝 Email Body
The **body** is the actual content of the email displayed to the recipient.  
It can include plain text, HTML formatting, or attachments depending on the type of email.  

---

## ✅ Summary
- Emails are divided into **Header** (metadata) and **Body** (content).  
- Headers contain critical fields like **From, To, Date**, plus optional fields for routing and identification.  
- The body delivers the actual message to the recipient.
