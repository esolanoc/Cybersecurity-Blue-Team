# 📧 How Email Works

---

## 📨 Email Structure
An email address has two parts:  

- **Mailbox** → `name`  
- **Domain** → `example.com`  

Example: `name@example.com`

---

## ⚙️ Email Protocols

### 🔹 SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol)
- Runs over **TCP port 25** for email transmission.  
- Secure/encrypted SMTP typically runs on **port 587**.  
- Used for **sending emails** between servers.  

### 🔹 POP3 (Post Office Protocol)
- Application protocol to **retrieve emails** from a server.  
- Allows users to **download emails** and read them locally.  

### 🔹 IMAP (Internet Mail Access Protocol)
- Enables accessing email from **multiple devices**.  
- Keeps messages stored on the server for synchronization.  

---

## 🌐 Webmail
Platforms like **Gmail** and **Outlook** allow users to access their email directly through a **web browser**.  
This provides convenience without needing a dedicated email client.  

---

## ✅ Summary
- Email addresses are split into **mailbox** and **domain**.  
- **SMTP** → Sending emails.  
- **POP3** → Downloading emails.  
- **IMAP** → Accessing emails across devices.  
- **Webmail** → Browser-based access (e.g., Gmail, Outlook).
