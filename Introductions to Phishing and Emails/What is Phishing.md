# 🎣 What is Phishing

---

## 📌 Definition
Phishing is a **social engineering attack** that attempts to:
- Steal personal or financial information.  
- Trick users into downloading malicious files.  
- Exploit trust through fake communication.  

---

## 🛠️ Techniques
Phishing commonly uses communication platforms such as:
- 📧 **Email** → Fake messages imitating trusted entities.  
- 💬 **SMS (Smishing)** → Text messages with malicious links.  
- 📞 **Phone calls (Vishing)** → Attackers impersonate legitimate organizations.  
- 🌐 **Internet/Websites** → Fraudulent sites designed to capture credentials.  

---

## 🚨 Key Characteristics
- Appears to come from a trusted source.  
- Creates urgency or fear (e.g., “Your account will be locked”).  
- Includes links or attachments leading to malware or credential theft.  

---

## ✅ Summary
Phishing is one of the most common cyber threats.  
It relies on **deception and manipulation** rather than technical exploits, making **awareness and training** critical defenses.
