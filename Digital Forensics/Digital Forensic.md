# 🕵️ Digital Forensics

Digital Forensics is the process of **collecting, analyzing, and preserving digital evidence** that can later be used in court during legal proceedings.  
It ensures that electronic evidence is handled properly to maintain its integrity and admissibility.

---

## 📌 The 5 Steps of Digital Forensics

### 1. 🔎 Identification
- Identify potential sources of relevant evidence or information.  
- Examples: hard drives, logs, emails, mobile devices.

---

### 2. 🛡️ Preservation
- Preserve relevant **Electronically Stored Information (ESI)**.  
- Protect the crime scene, capture visual images, and document all relevant info.  
- Maintain chain of custody.

---

### 3. 📥 Collection
- Gather digital information that may be relevant.  
- Ensure data is collected without altering the original evidence.
