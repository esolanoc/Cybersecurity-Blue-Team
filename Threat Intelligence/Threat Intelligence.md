# 🧠 Threat Intelligence

---

## 📌 Definition
Threat Intelligence is information that organizations use to **understand current and future threats**.  
It involves gathering **raw data** and transforming it into **actionable intelligence** that has real value for security teams.  

---

## 🔄 Threat Intelligence Phases

### 1. 📍 Planning and Direction
- Define the **scope** of the threat.  
- Set clear **goals**.  
- Identify **stakeholders** to keep the project on track.  

---

### 2. 📥 Collection
- Gather all possible information relevant to the threat.  
- Sources can include logs, threat feeds, OSINT, and internal telemetry.  

---

### 3. ⚙️ Processing
- Transform raw data into a **clear and readable format**.  
- Ensure the information is structured for analysis by security teams.  

---

### 4. 🔍 Analysis
- Human analysts interpret the processed data.  
- Identify patterns, adversary tactics, and potential impact.  

---

### 5. 📤 Dissemination
- Deliver the finished intelligence to where it is needed:  
  - SOC teams  
  - Incident response teams  
  - Security leadership  

---

### 6. 🔁 Feedback
- Collect regular feedback from the teams consuming the intelligence.  
- Adjust requirements and improve future intelligence cycles.  

---

## ✅ Summary
Threat Intelligence is about **turning data into actionable insights**.  
By following the phases — **Planning, Collection, Processing, Analysis, Dissemination, and Feedback** — organizations can stay ahead of adversaries and strengthen their defenses.
