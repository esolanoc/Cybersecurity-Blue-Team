# 🧩 Types of Intelligence

---

## 📌 Overview
Intelligence refers to the different methods used to **collect and analyze information** for security, defense, and strategic purposes.  
Each type of intelligence focuses on a specific source or method of data collection.  

---

## 🔹 SIGINT (Signals Intelligence)
Used to intercept **radio signals** or **broadcast communications**.  
Two subcategories:  
- **ELINT (Electronic Intelligence)** → Information gathered from electronic devices.  
- **COMINT (Communications Intelligence)** → Reading communications between groups and people (voice and messages).  

---

## 🔹 OSINT (Open Source Intelligence)
Information gathered from **publicly available resources**, such as:  
- Websites  
- Social media  
- News articles  
- Public records  

---

## 🔹 HUMINT (Human Intelligence)
Intelligence collected directly from **human sources**.  
Focuses on how people **think, act, and feel**.  
Examples: interviews, informants, or direct observation.  

---

## 🔹 GEOINT (Geospatial Intelligence)
Used to collect information about **geographic areas**, especially during:  
- Natural disasters  
- Wartime operations  
- Environmental monitoring  

---

## ✅ Summary
- **SIGINT** → Signals (ELINT + COMINT).  
- **OSINT** → Public sources.  
- **HUMINT** → Human interactions.  
- **GEOINT** → Geographic/environmental data.  

Each type of intelligence provides a **unique perspective** that, when combined, strengthens situational awareness and decision-making.
