# 📧 Email Security Controls

---

## 🚫 Spam Filters
These are pieces of software that scan incoming emails to detect telltale signs of spam or malicious content.  
They prevent such emails from being delivered to users' mailboxes.  

---

## 🔒 Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
A security control designed to prevent sensitive business or personal information from leaving the organization in an unauthorized manner.  

---

## 📨 Email Scanning
Emails can contain malicious attachments or URLs.  
Scanners identify threats using:  
- 📝 Pattern signatures  
- 📋 Blacklists of known senders  
- 🗂️ File hashes  
- 🌐 Malicious domain names  

---

## 🎓 Security Awareness Training
Mandatory training programs for new employees and routine refreshers for all staff.  
They teach users to:  
- 🔎 Analyze phishing emails  
- ⚠️ Recognize suspicious content  
- 🛡️ Take proper action when encountering threats
