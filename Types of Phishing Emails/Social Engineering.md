# 🎭 Social Engineering

Social engineering is the act of **convincing a victim to disclose confidential information** through **psychological manipulation** rather than technical methods.  
It exploits **trust, fear, curiosity, or urgency** to trick individuals into revealing sensitive data.

---

## 📌 Key Characteristics
- 🧠 Relies on **psychological methods** instead of hacking tools.  
- 🎯 Targets **human weaknesses** such as trust and authority.  
- ⚡ Often creates a **sense of urgency** to push quick decisions.  
- 👮 May impersonate trusted entities (banks, IT staff, managers).  

---

## ✨ Summary
Social engineering is one of the **most effective attack vectors** because it bypasses technical defenses and directly manipulates people.  
The best defense is **awareness, skepticism, and verification** before sharing any confidential information.
