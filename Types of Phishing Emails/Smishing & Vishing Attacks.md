# 📱 Smishing & Vishing Attacks

Smishing and vishing are **variants of phishing attacks** that use different communication vectors to trick victims into revealing sensitive information.

---

## 📩 Smishing
- Attack vector: **SMS or text message**.  
- Example: Fake bank alerts, delivery notifications, or prize messages.  
- Goal: Convince the victim to click a malicious link or share credentials.  

---

## 📞 Vishing
- Attack vector: **phone call**.  
- Victims: Often employees with access to **sensitive information**, typically one or two levels below the “C‑level” executives.  
- Goal: Trick the victim into **sharing passwords, financial data, or confidential details**.  

---

## 🛡️ Ways to Defend
- 📚 **User security awareness training**: Teach employees to verify before sharing information.  
- 🚫 **Block auto‑callers**: Helps reduce vishing attempts.  
- 🔑 **Internal authorization codes**: External attackers won’t know private codes.  
- 🧩 **Separation of duties**: Limits the number of people with access to sensitive actions (e.g., processing payments).  

---

## ✨ Summary
Smishing and vishing exploit **trust and urgency** through SMS or phone calls.  
The best defense is a mix of **education, technical controls, and organizational policies**.
