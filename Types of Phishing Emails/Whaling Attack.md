# 🐋 Whaling Attack

Whaling is a **highly-targeted phishing attack** that focuses on individuals in **management positions**, often **C‑level executives**.  
These executives are prime targets due to the **wealth of information** they have access to and the stereotype that they are **less educated around cybersecurity and phishing**.

---

## 🎯 Common Targets
- 👔 **Chief Operations Officer (COO)**  
- 👨‍💼 **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)**  
- 💰 **Chief Finance Officer (CFO)**  

---

## 📌 Key Characteristics
- Extremely **personalized and convincing** phishing emails.  
- Often mimic **business communications** (e.g., invoices, legal notices).  
- Aim to steal **credentials, financial data, or sensitive company information**.  

---

## 🛡️ Defense Strategies
- 📚 **Executive security awareness training**.  
- 🔍 **Email filtering and advanced threat detection**.
