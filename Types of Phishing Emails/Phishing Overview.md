# 🎣 Phishing Overview

Phishing is one of the **most common types of cyberattacks** because it targets **human weaknesses** to steal credentials that can later be used to gain access to services or accounts.

---

## 📌 Key Points

- 🕸️ Uses **fake websites** that look like legitimate ones.  
- 🎭 Relies on **social engineering tactics** such as:  
  - ⚡ **Sense of urgency** (e.g., "Your account will be locked today!")  
  - 👮 **False authority** (e.g., pretending to be a bank or IT admin).  

---

## ✨ Summary
Phishing attacks exploit **trust and emotions** rather than technical flaws.  
The best defense is **awareness and verification** before clicking links or entering credentials.
