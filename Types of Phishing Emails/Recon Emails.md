# 📧 Recon Emails

Recon emails are used to check if destination mailboxes are active, often as a **precursor to phishing attacks**.  
They help attackers identify valid email addresses before launching more complex campaigns.

---

## 🔎 Types of Recon Emails

### 1. 🗑️ Recon Spam
- Emails generated with **random letters**.  
- Example: `"ajshjfbagb"`  
- Purpose: Test if the mailbox exists and accepts delivery.

---

### 2. 🎭 Social Engineering Emails
- Crafted to **trick the recipient into responding**.  
- May pretend to be a legitimate sender.  
- Goal: Confirm the mailbox is monitored by a human.
